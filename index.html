MyActuary

Personal mortality assessment

Your own actuarial life table, built from the evidence.

Answer about 40 questions on health and habits. MyActuary starts from the Social Security Administration's latest U.S. period life table and adjusts each year's probability of death using hazard ratios from large peer-reviewed studies. You get year-by-year death probabilities, a survival curve, life expectancy, and the factors costing you the most years.

  • Private by design. No accounts, no analytics, no server. A browser security policy on this page blocks all network requests.
  • Saved only in this tab. Answers live in session storage and disappear when you close the tab. Export a PDF to keep them.
  • Cited. Every adjustment links to the study it comes from.
Not medical or financial advice. MyActuary is an educational estimate based on population averages. It cannot account for everything about your health and is no substitute for a clinician, a licensed insurance underwriter, or a financial planner.
